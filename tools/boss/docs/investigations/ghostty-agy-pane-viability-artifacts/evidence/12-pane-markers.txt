Pane text markers, counted per poll and per phase (tools.py markers)
====================================================================

Phases come from hook timestamps, not from the screen: 'busy' runs from the PreInvocation that opens an execution
to its Stop (or to the Escape keypress for an interrupted turn); 'bg_wait' runs from a Stop with fullyIdle=false
until the agent is invoked again; 'starting' is everything before the session's first hook. Polls within 600 ms of
a phase edge are dropped. Cells are hits/polls.
